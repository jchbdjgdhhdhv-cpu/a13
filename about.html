About

Made by people who press the pusher just to watch the hand move.

Chronos is an independent website about chronograph watches.

We explain how chronographs work and how to read every scale on their dials, in plain language. Our tools let you try a stopwatch and a tachymeter in the browser before you ever wear one.

We do not sell watches, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
